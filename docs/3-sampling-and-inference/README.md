# Sampling & Inference

Probabilistic inference with `jax-fli`: a NumPyro forward model, custom MCMC distributions, reparameterisation of bounded parameters, and field-level posteriors over the cosmology and the initial-condition field.

- [Probabilistic Modeling](12-Probabilistic-Modeling.ipynb) — the forward-model builder, the `Configurations` dataclass, custom MCMC distributions, and the NumPyro/BlackJAX wrappers.
- [Rosenbrock](13-Rosen.ipynb) — a sanity check of the samplers (NUTS, MCLMC and MAMS at a matched gradient budget) on a known target, before any cosmology.
- [LPT mass mapping](14-LPT-MassMapping.ipynb) — MAP reconstruction of the initial-condition field from the convergence maps of DES Y3 bins 2 and 3 (2LPT spherical lightcone, 416³ on one A100), at the DES Y3 source density (Part I) and at the Euclid IST:F density of 30 galaxies/arcmin², 7.5 per bin (Part II). The IC is compared through its lensing-kernel sky projection, with the κ cross-correlation against the Wiener limit and starlet statistics. [Experiment 13](../5-experiments/13-map-lpt2-mass-mapping/README.md) draws its figures from these runs.
- [Multi-host LPT mass mapping](15-LPT-Multihost-MassMapping.py) — the script counterpart of 14, running the same MAP reconstruction over a multi-host device mesh (one process per GPU via `jax.distributed`) and writing the same parquet layout, so the plotting sections of the notebook read its output unchanged. Its 1200³ DES Y3 run on 32 GPUs is the main run of [Experiment 13](../5-experiments/13-map-lpt2-mass-mapping/README.md), with [the animation](../5-experiments/13-map-lpt2-mass-mapping/animation/map_run.gif) of its reconstruction.
- [LPT field-level inference of the initial conditions](16-LPT-FieldLevel-IC.ipynb) — the Euclid model of notebook 14 (2LPT spherical lightcone, two κ maps) sampled with MCLMC over the joint posterior of `(Ω_c, σ₈)` and the initial-condition field at 128³ on one A100: the IC in 3D and in lensing-kernel projection (truth, mean, standard deviation, residual), its transfer function and coherence, the posterior-predictive κ with its coherence and transfer function, and the starlet ℓ₁ norm.
- [LPT field-level inference of the cosmology](17-LPT-FieldLevel-Cosmology.ipynb) — the model of notebook 16 with its own mock data, conditioned on the true white-noise IC: NUTS on `(Ω_c, σ₈)` alone gives the conditional cosmology posterior, cross-checked on a grid of the log-posterior.
- [Configuration options](configurations-options.md) — the `Configurations` fields that drive the forward model.

The outputs of notebooks 14, 16 and 17 and of the 1200³ run of script 15 are published in the [`ASKabalan/jax-fli-sampling`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ASKabalan/jax-fli-sampling) dataset.
